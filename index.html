Plan guide

The rules behind the numbers. Read once, then let the app do the nagging.

Lean as fast as possible, no shortcuts

  • The deficit is set at the fastest safe pace: about 650 kcal a day under maintenance, 0.6 to 0.7 kg a week. Going lower than your floor (shown in the warnings) is the shortcut that backfires: you lose muscle, look softer at the same weight, and rebound.
  • Protein is non-negotiable: around 1.9 g per kg. It is the difference between "lost weight" and "look lean".
  • Lift 4 days, walk every day. 8,000 to 10,000 steps. Cardio alone makes you a smaller version of the same shape.
  • No cheat days, one treat meal. The app flags a second one.
  • Milk: the plan assumes 3% milk. Whole or 6% milk has double the fat for the same protein, keep it for an occasional glass.
  • Sleep 7 hrs. Under 6 hrs the body drops muscle instead of fat on the same diet.
  • Check-in every 2 weeks: if the 7-day average hasn't moved for 2 weeks, cut 100 kcal from carbs, never from protein. If you feel flat and weak for 3 days straight, eat at maintenance for one day and continue.

Breakfast rotation (one rule: protein on the plate)

  • Non-veg days: any Indian breakfast + 2 eggs. Idli + eggs, dosa + eggs, poha + eggs, upma + eggs, bread + eggs. Eggs are the protein, the Indian item is the carb.
  • Veg days: any Indian breakfast + a whey shake. Pesarattu + whey is the best of them (36 g protein). Upma + curd also works.
  • Overnight oats with chia and berries, or oats + whey + chia + berries: the highest protein, zero morning effort. Granola with milk 2 or 3 times a week, 30 to 40 g.
  • Fruit for breakfast only with protein beside it: banana + Greek yogurt + nuts, or fruit bowl + whey. A fruit-only breakfast is 300 kcal of sugar and 5 g protein, you'll be hungry by 10.
  • Bread + maple syrup, pancakes: treats. Sunday, once, with 2 eggs beside them so the meal still has protein.
  • Nuts: 15 to 20 g on top of oats, granola or yogurt. Never from the bag.

Cheap protein (when eggs and chicken cost too much)

  • $ per 30 g protein, cheapest first: whey (big bag from a warehouse store), soya chunks, dal and chana, milk, canned sardines, curd. Then egg whites by the carton, eggs, Greek yogurt and skyr, tofu, frozen basa, chicken legs and thighs. Chicken breast, fish fillets, prawns, mutton and paneer are the expensive ones.
  • Veggie salad is free food, eat it with every lunch and dinner. But it counts toward protein only if it carries some: add 2 eggs, 150 g tofu, 200 g chana, sprouted moong or 80 g paneer. A plain salad is 35 kcal and 1 g protein, it fills the plate, not the target.
  • The swap sheet shows $, $$ or $$$ beside each protein. The warning panel suggests the cheapest way to close a protein gap.

Daily cooking, fresh (about 45 min total)

  • Morning, 10 min: lime water on, boil the eggs while oats cook in milk. Chia and berries go in cold. That is breakfast for two.
  • Lunch, 20 min, one pot: onion, tomato, ginger-garlic in 2 tsp peanut oil, add the protein (chicken, or soya / paneer on veg days), add 300 g chopped veg, salt, turmeric, chilli, coriander, lid on 12 min. Rice in the cooker at the same time. Serve the bigger portion to the higher target.
  • Dinner, 15 min, one pan: tofu or chicken-mushroom, same base, higher heat, no lid. Rice 100 g each from lunch's cooker is fine for the same day.
  • The only things worth making ahead: dal (2 days), boiled chana (3 days), chicken marinated in curd + masala the night before (cooks faster, tastes better). Everything else is fresh.
  • Chop once a day: cut all the veg for lunch and dinner in the morning, two containers, done.
  • Freezer staples for the days you can't cook: frozen mixed veg, peas, spinach, berries. Tofu-veg stir-fry from frozen veg is 8 minutes.

Food rules

  • If the ring is amber, add whey, Greek yogurt or egg whites. Red means fix it before you eat the next meal.
  • All weights are cooked weight except whey, oats, granola, poha, rava and roasted chana (dry).
  • Curries: 1 tsp cold-pressed peanut oil per serving (olive oil on salads, coconut oil or ghee only as a 1 tsp swap). Every oil and ghee is 45 kcal a teaspoon, the type doesn't matter for fat loss, the amount does. Oil is the hidden 300 kcal in home cooking. Puri, biryani and pasta sit in the Treat group: one per week, enter it and move on.
  • Each meal shows protein eaten / protein needed (green = met, amber = close, red = short). Breakfast 18%, mid-morning 12%, lunch 28%, pre-gym 15%, dinner 27% of your daily target. When the whole day is on target every meal shows green; when the day is short, the red meal is the one to fix first, with a bigger portion of the protein already in it.
  • Ate something different? + Add food → Custom. Pick ingredients and enter grams or pieces (e.g. 100 ml 6% milk, 40 g granola, 20 blueberries) and it is logged with exact numbers and saved under My foods for next time.
  • Meals are kept to 1 or 2 items. Lunch and dinner are a one-pot protein-and-veg curry plus one carb. Breakfast is 2 items, snacks are 1. Salad is free, add it when you want it. Use − and + on any item to change the portion in half steps. Swap stays inside the same group. Add food builds a meal from scratch.
  • Craving kit: dark chocolate 2 squares, chai with 1/2 tsp sugar, fruit, or curd with cinnamon. Keep the pantry boring.
  • 10 min walk after lunch and dinner. 3 L water. 7 hrs sleep. Weigh every morning, judge the weekly average.
