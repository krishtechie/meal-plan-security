Privacy

Last updated November 2026

Meal Planner is a small, self-hosted app for logging meals, water, sleep and weight within a household. This page says exactly what it stores and what it does with it.

What is stored

  • Account: your name, e-mail address and a bcrypt hash of your password. Never the password itself.
  • Profile: the things you enter in setup or Profiles: body type, age, height, current and goal weight, activity level, goal and pace, how you eat (veg, non-veg, eggs, veg days, foods to avoid), cuisines, and the calorie, protein, carb, fat and step targets computed from them.
  • Logs: the foods you add to each meal, portions, water, bedtime and wake time, weight entries, custom foods and saved meal templates.
  • Sessions and activity: a hash of each device's session token with its IP address and browser type, and an activity log of API requests (time, IP, action, result). Never food data or passwords.

Who can see it

  • You, and the other members of your household. The household owner can see and edit every member's logs and profile; members see the household but edit only their own.
  • The person who runs this site has technical access to the server and database, as with any self-hosted service. They do not look at household data in normal operation and have no reason to.
  • Nobody else. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no data sales.

Where it lives

In one SQLite database file on the server that hosts this site, outside the web root, reachable only through the app's API over HTTPS. Hosting is on a shared cPanel host in Canada.

E-mail

Three kinds of e-mail only: confirm your address, reset your password, and household invites. They are sent through Brevo (Sendinblue), which sees the recipient address, subject and message body for delivery. No marketing mail, ever.

Your controls

  • Export: Settings → Export gives you everything as a spreadsheet or CSV.
  • Devices: Settings → Signed-in devices lists every session and lets you end any of them.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account removes your account and all your logs immediately. If you are the household owner it removes the whole household. There is no retention period.

Cookies and storage

No cookies. The app keeps your session token and a cache of your own data in your browser's local storage so it works offline; Sign out clears it.

Age

The app is for adults. Accounts require confirming you are 18 or older, and the target calculator refuses an age under 18.

Source

The code is public at github.com/krishtechie/meal-plan-security, including the security fixes made along the way.
